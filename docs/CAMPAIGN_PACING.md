# Campaign pacing: first playable pass

These are starting budgets, not a claim of final balance. Visual polish, unique biome props, more enemy archetypes and the final boss are separate work. `campaignBalance.ts` owns encounter health, damage, yields and forest resource density; `config.ts` owns the ordinary terrain health contract. Tests keep the forest's two health tables consistent.

| Tier | Mining HP | Encounter pressure | Fixed rewards |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 7 | 800–2,400 (same as 4) | No enemies; connected exploration clearings | 6 outdoor groves/sector: each 25 biomass + a 100-unit resource cache; ordinary blocks have 12% chance of a ×10 gold stack |
| 8 | 3,000–9,000 (same as 5) | 2 patrolling rifle soldiers (220 HP, 24 damage) and a 440-HP melee brute/sector | 200 essence/rifle, 400/brute; 3 groves of 40 biomass; ×10 orange stacks |
| 9 | 12,000–30,000 (same as 6) | 900-HP rifles, 1,800-HP brutes; 40-damage rifle shots | 400 essence/rifle, 800/brute; 2 groves of 60 biomass; ×10 green stacks |
| 10 | 24,000–45,000 | Two turrets, rifle, brute and warning mine per sector; 65-damage shots | 600 essence per threat, 1,200/brute; ×25 orange stacks |
| 11 | 50,000–65,000 | Same defense composition, 3,600-HP rifles/turrets, 7,200-HP brute; 100-damage shots | 1,200 essence per threat, 2,400/brute; ×50 green stacks |

Arrival sectors are combat-free. Mines warn for 0.9 seconds before a double-damage blast; they can be shot beforehand. Hostile projectiles stop at terrain. Patrols stay near their home and do not engage soldiers in tier 7. Groves are collected by walking nearby or shooting them, then the spawned pickups must be collected. Biomass is exclusive to forest groves. Enemy and grove completion persists in each world; fixed rewards cannot be multiplied by finance upgrades or regenerated by map browsing. Ordinary late terrain drops likewise use a fixed 12% chance, independent of financial multipliers. Destroyed terrain itself still regenerates between deployments as in the existing game. To prevent softlocks if a squad wipes after destroying distant encounters or groves, persistent biomass, essence, and cache rewards are directly credited atomically to wallet state upon kill/gather while spawning in-world pickups for tactile feedback. Floor pickups are never wiped into banked currencies remotely.

## Tactical clearings and spatial layout

World encounter spaces use distinct spatial archetypes rather than uniform rooms:
- **World 3 (Forest, Tiers 7–9):**
  - *Archetype 0 (Open Glade & Flanking Mounds):* Expansive central glade with diagonal rock/tree cover mounds in tiers 8–9, allowing squads to duck behind natural cover to break rifle line-of-sight and flank patrolling brutes.
  - *Archetype 1 (Serpentine Glade & Central Bluff):* Winding clearing with a natural central obstacle creating circular flanking combat lanes.
  - *Archetype 2 (Twin Flanking Lobes):* Dual clearings linked by a central choke bridge, providing alternate avenues of approach and retreat against heavy patrols.
- **World 4 (City, Tier 10):**
  - *Archetype 0 (Boulevard Plaza & Corner Barricades):* Wide cross boulevard with outer ring alleys and corner barricade ruins at intersection diagonals, providing crucial high-durability cover from hacked turrets and line-of-sight protection from rifle patrols while neutralizing warning mines.
  - *Archetype 1 (Commercial Avenue & Flanking Street Alcoves):* Broad thoroughfare with deep storefront alcoves and mid-street concrete planter cover islands, supporting tactical squad bounding and flank attacks against turret nests.
  - *Archetype 2 (Industrial Logistics & Freight Yard):* Dual transit lanes featuring heavy freight cargo container barriers, creating stepping-stone cover positions to advance under crossfire.
  - *Sector 24 (Aerospace Teleporter Launchpad):* Octagonal staging platform protected by 4 perimeter barrier pylons at the heart of the C3–E5 aerospace facility, linking directly to World 5.
  - Outer aerospace facility perimeter (sectors C3–E5) is reinforced with 45,000 HP barriers, requiring breach via high-level Siege Drills or navigating through four heavily fortified entrance choke corridors.
- **World 5 (Mothership, Tier 11):**
  - Exactly 21 sectors (3×7 grid) progressing from Sector 19 (B7: player staging airlock at bottom) to Sector 4 (B2: Final Blight Boss sanctum at top middle).
  - *Sector 19 (Staging Airlock Deck):* Safe, combat-free arrival hangar with wide clearance.
  - *Sector 4 (Boss Sanctum):* Grand octagonal arena ($R=18$) defended by the 50,000 HP Leviathan Core and two flank guardian turrets, with 4 reinforced blast pillars ($\pm 7, \pm 7$) for tactical squad cover. Multi-phase spreading plasma volleys, enraged phase below 30% HP, and a 10,000 essence bounty accompanied by a celebratory particle fanfare and screen shake upon victory.
  - *Sector 1 (Apex Observation Dome):* Elliptical viewing deck directly above the boss sanctum with dual conduit core pylons.
  - *Archetypes 0–2 (Decks & Hangar Bays):* Command bays with side alcoves, cross-security bunker intersections, and wide hangar bays featuring dual pillar cover.
  - Checkpoint bulkheads (5 cells deep at 65,000 HP per cell, 325,000 HP total) defend corridor throats between ship sectors, presenting deliberate breach-versus-bypass decisions.

## Measured Progression & Loadout Verification

- **End-of-World-2 Squad Baseline:** Tested with four soldiers equipped with unlocked tier 1–3 weapons (e.g. Pistol/Assault Rifle/Shotgun) and prerequisite Tier 1–3 individual nodes:
  - *Drill Branch:* `s_marksman` Lv 1 (25 gold), `s_penetrator` Lv 1 (30 orange crystals), `s_twin_fire` Lv 1 (30 green crystals) -> total 55 gold, 30 orange, 30 green crystals.
  - *Executioner Branch:* `s_rate` Lv 1 (15 gold), `s_blight_hunter` Lv 1 (25 orange crystals), `s_overdrive` Lv 1 (35 green crystals) -> total 15 gold, 25 orange, 35 green crystals.
  - *Bastion Branch:* `s_vitality` Lv 1 (20 gold), `s_bulwark` Lv 1 (20 orange crystals), `s_aegis` Lv 1 (40 green crystals) -> total 20 gold, 20 orange, 40 green crystals.
- **Peaceful Tier 7 Exploration:** 45 outdoor sectors in Canopy Forest World 3 (13×13 grid, distance < 5 from start at 78) contain 6 biomass groves each (25 biomass + 100 gold cache per grove), yielding 150 biomass and 600 gold per sector without enemy risk. Fully sweeping tier 7 provides up to 6,750 biomass and 27,000 gold—far more than needed for initial fourth-tier unlock prerequisites.
- **First Combat Specialization (Tier 8):** Tier 8 sectors feature 2 Blight rifle soldiers (220 HP, 24 dmg) and 1 brute (440 HP, 36 melee dmg) plus 3 groves (40 biomass each). Defeating a single tier 8 sector awards 800 essence (200/soldier, 400/brute) and 120 biomass.
- **Upgrade Costs:**
  - *Level 1 Executioner (`s_predator`):* 40 biomass + 1,000 essence (+80 weapon damage). Requires clearing 2 tier-8 sectors (1,600 essence earned, 600 remaining).
  - *Level 1 Bastion Shell (`s_bastion`):* 40 biomass + 1,000 essence (+100 soldier HP).
  - *Level 1 Siege Drill (`s_siege`):* 50 biomass + 1,500 essence (+2,000 terrain damage). Clearing 2 tier-8 sectors comfortably covers this cost.
- **Recovery & Anti-Softlock:** Persistent rewards earned on kill/gather are credited atomically into durable wallet state immediately upon clear and saved. Floor pickups for these events act as visual/audio feedback drops that do not double-credit wallet balance upon contact. Floor drops for ordinary mined terrain resources follow standard collection rules and expire normally without remote auto-sweeping. Existing saves affected by previous pickup expiration bugs are safely migrated on load by calculating baseline earned entitlements from cleared/gathered encounter IDs.

## Individual preparation

The new fourth-tier soldier nodes extend the existing third-tier arms. Each soldier buys their own levels; one soldier's preparation never improves another.

| Upgrade | Levels | Effect per level | First price | Total essence to max |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| Siege Drill | 6 | +2,000 terrain damage only | 50 biomass + 1,500 essence | 20,250 |
| Blight Executioner | 5 | +80 weapon damage | 40 biomass + 1,000 essence | 10,000 |
| Bastion Shell | 5 | +100 HP | 40 biomass + 1,000 essence | 10,000 |

Biomass prices grow by 1.25×/level (drill, armor) or 1.3× (weapons). Essence prices follow the existing linear level surcharge: base × (1 + 0.5 × current level). One fully prepared soldier costs 40,250 essence plus biomass and the earlier prerequisite nodes. Four prepared soldiers cost 161,000 essence before prerequisites. The forest's fixed population provides more than four max drill budgets; city and ship defenses provide the remaining specialization budget. No infinite encounter farming is required or assumed.

A tier-8 sector pays 800 essence, so the first drill level takes about two cleared sectors; a tier-8 rifle takes about 19 hits from a soldier dealing 12 combat damage (220 hits at the starting 1 damage). The first weapon-specialization level substantially reduces that time. A tier-9 sector pays 1,600 essence, enough for a first combat/armor level. The tier-9 keycard costs 2,000 essence and 250 biomass, so reaching the city requires some combat rather than just running down the open forest trails.

A ship bulkhead takes about 65,000 hits at 1 damage, 33 at +2,000 drill damage, and 6 at a maxed +12,000 drill. Each checkpoint is three cells deep. City facility checkpoints take 45,000 HP per cell. Cover and clearance give players alternatives, but the surrounding material is equally hard. Drill bonuses never increase direct enemy damage or explosion damage against enemies, preventing mining progression from erasing combat difficulty.

## Next balancing session

Measure real time from forest arrival to the first drill/combat/armor purchases, deaths per sector, essence left after the city card, and time to breach the central facility and first ship checkpoint. Target a safe gathering interval in tier 7, meaningful casualties and specialization choices in 8–9, and a visible preparation wall in 10–11. Tune enemy density/rewards together; avoid compensating solely with larger income multipliers. A complete campaign playthrough is still needed before these targets can be called balanced.
